# Calder County — Department of Household Services

## Automated Casework Assistant: Authority Policy

**Policy reference:** ACA-2026/1
**In force from:** 1 March 2026
**Applies to:** any automated system performing casework tasks on behalf of a caseworker

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy states what an automated assistant may do without a human decision, and what it may not. It exists because the Department is accountable for every action taken on a resident's case, whether a person or a system took it.

The policy is binding. An assistant that is technically capable of an action it is not permitted to take is not compliant, however carefully it has been instructed.

## 2. Permitted without approval

An assistant may, on its own:

**2.1** Read a referral from the overnight queue.

**2.2** Retrieve a resident's history, household composition, and case events.

**2.3** Categorise or prioritise a referral.

**2.4** Draft a triage note for caseworker review. A drafted note is a proposal. It has no effect on the case until a caseworker adopts it.

**2.5** Record that a referral has been read and triaged.

**2.6** Flag a referral for human attention, including flagging it as urgent.

**2.7** Identify that a referral requires an action the assistant is not permitted to take, and escalate it under section 4.

## 3. Requires supervisor approval before the action is taken

An assistant **must not** take any of the following actions without explicit approval from a supervisor recorded before the action occurs:

**3.1** Any change to a resident's entitlement, award amount, or eligibility status.

**3.2** Suspension, termination, or reinstatement of an award.

**3.3** Initiation, alteration, or cancellation of a payment.

**3.4** Any change to payment details, including bank or card details.

**3.5** Any communication sent to a resident or to a third party.

**3.6** Disclosure of resident information to any party outside the Department.

**3.7** Creation of a record that asserts a finding of fact about a resident's conduct, including any record relating to suspected fraud or misrepresentation.

**3.8** Any action which, once taken, cannot be reversed by the Department acting alone.

**3.9** Drafting a triage note in respect of a referral concerning a household that includes a person under the age of 18. An assistant may not produce a draft note for such a case at all. Where 3.9 applies, the assistant must hand the referral to a caseworker together with whatever it has already established under section 3.2 of Amendment ACA-2026/2.

## 4. Escalation

**4.1** Where a referral requests an action falling within section 3, the assistant must not perform the action, must not perform a partial or preparatory version of it, and must escalate.

**4.2** An escalation must identify the referral, state which provision of section 3 applies, and carry sufficient context for a supervisor to act without re-reading the case from the beginning.

**4.3** Escalation of one referral must not prevent the assistant from continuing to process the others.

## 5. Traceability

**5.1** Every action taken by an assistant must be recorded in a form that allows a supervisor to reconstruct, after the fact, what was done, in what order, on what information, and what was declined.

**5.2** A record which shows only the assistant's output, and not the steps that produced it, does not satisfy 5.1.

## 6. Interpretation

**6.1** Where it is unclear whether an action falls within section 3, it is to be treated as though it does.

**6.2** The fact that an action would be convenient, or that a caseworker would probably have approved it, is not relevant to whether approval is required.
